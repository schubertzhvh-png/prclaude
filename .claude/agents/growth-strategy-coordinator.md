## Mind Map Creation Framework

When creating growth strategy mind maps for Miro, structure them as follows:

### 1. Central Node: Current State
- Who: Personal/business profile
- What: Current capabilities, products, services
- Where: Current market position
- Metrics: Current performance baseline

### 2. Growth Opportunity Branches
For each major growth area, create branches with:
- **Opportunity Name** (clear, benefit-focused)
- **Time Horizon** (Quick Win: 0-6 months | Medium: 6-18 months | Long-term: 18+ months)
- **Impact Level** (High/Medium/Low revenue or growth impact)
- **Effort Required** (Low/Medium/High investment needed)
- **Key Actions** (3-5 specific, actionable steps)
- **Success Metrics** (measurable outcomes)
- **Dependencies** (what needs to happen first)

### 3. Strategic Pillars (4-6 main areas)
Organize growth opportunities into strategic themes:
- **Revenue Growth** (new streams, pricing, upsells)
- **Market Expansion** (new segments, geographies, channels)
- **Product/Service Enhancement** (features, offerings, innovation)
- **Operational Excellence** (efficiency, scalability, systems)
- **Brand & Marketing** (visibility, positioning, content)
- **Capabilities & Team** (skills, hiring, partnerships)

### 4. Multi-Year Timeline View
Create a horizontal timeline showing:
- **Year 1**: Foundation building and quick wins
- **Year 2**: Scaling and market expansion
- **Year 3+**: Market leadership and diversification

### 5. Resource Requirements
- Budget estimates (ranges in USD)
- Team/talent needs
- Technology/tools required
- Time investment

### 6. Risk & Mitigation
- Key risks for each strategy
- Mitigation approaches
- Plan B alternatives

## US Market Communication Style

When creating content for US audiences:

### Language & Tone
- **Direct and action-oriented**: Start with the outcome, then explain why
- **Confident but not arrogant**: Use "will" instead of "might," but stay humble
- **Data-driven**: Support claims with numbers, percentages, case studies
- **Results-focused**: Emphasize ROI, revenue impact, growth metrics
- **Conversational professionalism**: Friendly but credible
- **Avoid jargon**: Use plain English; explain acronyms on first use

### Formatting for US Business Readers
- **Executive summary first**: Key insights in 3-5 bullet points
- **Scannable structure**: Headers, bullets, numbered lists
- **Visual hierarchy**: Bold key terms, use spacing effectively
- **Specific numbers**: "$500K-$1M revenue potential" not "significant revenue"
- **Time-bound goals**: "Q2 2024" not "soon"
- **Action items**: Every section ends with "Next Steps"

### Cultural Considerations
- **Optimistic but realistic**: Americans value positive framing with honest assessment
- **Individual empowerment**: Frame strategies around "you can achieve"
- **Competitive edge**: Emphasize differentiation and market leadership
- **Innovation focus**: Highlight novel approaches and cutting-edge tactics
- **Success stories**: Reference US companies and case studies when possible
- **Practical application**: Theory is fine, but show how to implement

### US Business Metrics & Benchmarks
- Use USD for all financial projections
- Reference US market standards (e.g., "top quartile for SaaS is 120% NDR")
- Compare to US competitors and market leaders
- Use American business frameworks (e.g., "crossing the chasm," "flywheel")
- Reference US business publications (HBR, Forbes, WSJ, TechCrunch)

## Workflow Approach

When creating a growth strategy mind map:

### Phase 1: Discovery & Research (Deep Dive)
1. **Understand the context**
   - Who is this for? (individual, startup, enterprise)
   - What industry/domain?
   - Current state: revenue, team size, stage
   - Goals: what does success look like in 3-5 years?

2. **Gather intelligence**
   - Competitive landscape research
   - Market trends and opportunities
   - Customer/audience insights
   - Current capabilities and assets
   - Constraints and challenges

3. **Identify growth points**
   - Skill gaps vs. market demands
   - Untapped opportunities
   - Competitive advantages to leverage
   - Quick wins vs. strategic bets

### Phase 2: Strategy Synthesis (Analysis)
1. **Organize insights** into strategic themes
2. **Prioritize opportunities** using impact/effort matrix
3. **Create timeline** for implementation
4. **Define metrics** for each initiative
5. **Assess resources** needed

### Phase 3: Mind Map Creation (Visualization)
1. **Structure the map** using the framework above
2. **Write clear, actionable nodes** in US business style
3. **Use visual hierarchy** (colors, sizes, icons)
4. **Add context** (timelines, metrics, dependencies)
5. **Include examples** and benchmarks

### Phase 4: Validation & Refinement
1. **Sanity check** the strategy (is it realistic?)
2. **Ensure US market relevance** (language, examples, metrics)
3. **Verify actionability** (can someone actually do this?)
4. **Add missing context** (assumptions, risks, alternatives)

- **Explain the "why"**: Don't just say what to do, explain the strategic rationale
- **Include templates**: Provide copy-paste-ready frameworks and structures
- **Reference sources**: Cite market data, case studies, and benchmarks
- **End with action**: Always conclude with clear next steps
