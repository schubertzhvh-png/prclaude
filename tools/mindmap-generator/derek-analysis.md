### Instagram Optimization

**Current Issues:**
- 0.97% engagement (below 1.5% minimum)
- Scattered content themes
- Lifestyle-heavy (not authority-building)

**Fixes:**

1. **Content Buckets (70/20/10 Rule):**

   - 20% Authority: Hot takes, thought leadership
   - 10% Personal: Behind-scenes, lifestyle

2. **Reels Strategy:**
   - Hook-first (3 seconds to grab attention)
   - Value-dense (teach 1 thing clearly)
   - CTA: "Follow for more agency tips"
   - Post 5-7 Reels/week
   - Best times: Mon/Wed/Fri 11am, Tue/Thu 2pm

3. **Feed Posts:**
   - Carousel posts (swipe = engagement boost)
   - 3-5 posts/week
   - Alt text for accessibility (SEO boost)
   - Consistent visual brand

4. **Stories:**
   - Daily engagement (polls, questions, quizzes)
   - Behind-scenes of client work
   - Promote lead magnet 3-5x/week
   - Story highlights: About, Testimonials, Free Training, Resources

5. **Engagement Tactics:**
   - Reply to every DM within 24 hours
   - Comment on 20-30 target accounts daily
   - Share audience DMs (with permission)
   - Host weekly Q&A sessions

### YouTube Optimization

**Current Issues:**
- Only 1.1K subs (small)
- Low-medium engagement
- Mixed content (education + lifestyle)

**Fixes:**

1. **SEO-Driven Content:**
   - Keyword research before every video
   - Target: "how to scale agency", "agency systems", "agency profit"
   - Title format: "[Benefit] in [Timeframe] ([Social Proof])"
   - Example: "How to Scale Your Agency to $1M in 12 Months (5 Proven Systems)"

2. **Thumbnail Strategy:**
   - Face + emotion (curiosity, surprise, success)
   - Bold text (3-5 words max)
   - Consistent branding (color scheme)
   - Test A/B variants

3. **Video Structure:**
   - Hook (0-15 sec): State the benefit
   - Preview (15-30 sec): What they'll learn
   - Content (3-15 min): Deliver value
   - CTA (last 30 sec): Lead magnet or subscribe

4. **Upload Schedule:**
   - 1-2 videos/week minimum
   - Same days (consistency = algorithm boost)
   - Shorts: Repurpose Reels (3-5/week)

5. **Playlists:**
   - "Agency Scaling Systems"
   - "Agency Profitability"
   - "Client Acquisition"
   - "Team Building"
   - (Increases watch time = more reach)
